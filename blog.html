How to Make Your Printer Last Longer

VS By Vidyut Shah 10 Min Read

In today's fast-paced business environment, your printer is more than just a peripheral—it's a critical operational node. At Vijayratna Enterprises, we've seen how proper care can extend service life by years.

1. Precision Toner Management

Using high-density premium powder in your cartridges isn't just about print quality; it's about fuser health. Poor quality toner can cause buildup on the Teflon surface, leading to early failure of the heating element.

Did You Know?

Regular cleaning of the pickup rollers with specialized solution can prevent 80% of common paper jam protocols.

2. Environmental Calibration

Printers are sensitive to particulate matter and humidity. Ensuring your hardware operates in a climate-controlled environment reduces the static charge on the drum unit, ensuring consistently sharp output.
